Study Better Econ

Upload an economics lecture. Get notes, then see them in the real world.

Your slides become study notes first. Real-world examples, each with a source link, follow a couple of minutes later.

  • Notes on up to three topics, with an analogy where a topic is hard.
  • News articles that show each topic in real events.
  • United States and India side by side, using World Bank data.
  • Modern history episodes where the same idea played out.

Your slides are sent to Google's Gemini to write the notes. Upload only slides you are fine sharing. Notes are deleted after 24 hours.
